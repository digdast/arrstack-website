Plex Media Server

Radarr (Movies)

Automated movie collection manager. Monitors RSS feeds for new movie releases, connects to indexers, grabs torrents, and auto-upgrades video formats to 4K REMUX.

Sonarr (TV Shows)

Automated television series manager. Tracks upcoming series episodes, seasons, anime, downloads new airings automatically, and moves them to your Plex TV directory.

Prowlarr (Indexers)

Centralized indexer and tracker proxy. Configure your public and private torrent trackers once, and Prowlarr synchronizes them automatically with both Sonarr and Radarr.
